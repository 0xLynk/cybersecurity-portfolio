## Reflection

Competing in NCL reinforced a few things for me:

- **Teamwork multiplies results.** Our strongest categories came from matching people to the work they do best and trusting each other to deliver.
- **Breadth matters.** The competition covers nine areas, from OSINT and cryptography to forensics and web exploitation. Even when you specialize, understanding the other areas helps you collaborate and spot connections.
- **Time management is a skill.** Deciding when to keep pushing on a problem and when to move on directly affects the final score.
- **The gaps are the roadmap.** Our scores show where we were strongest and where there is room to grow. Web application exploitation and some of the harder reconnaissance and exploitation work are my next focus areas.
